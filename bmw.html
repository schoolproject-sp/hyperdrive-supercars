Motorsport Heritage & Nürburgring Testing

BMW M GmbH is the high-performance division of German automaker BMW AG, originally created to power BMW's championship-winning 1960s and 1970s racing programs.

  • Track-Tuned Engineering: Every M-badged vehicle features upgraded twin-turbo engines, adaptive M suspension, carbon-ceramic brakes, and aerodynamic body kits.
  • Nürburgring Proving Ground: All M models undergo thousands of kilometers of high-speed testing at BMW's private facility at the Nürburgring Nordschleife in Germany.
  • Lightweight Carbon Craft: Flagship models like the M4 CSL use carbon-fiber-reinforced plastic roofs, hoods, and bucket seats to cut weight and sharpen cornering agility.
