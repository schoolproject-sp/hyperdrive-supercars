Milestones That Changed Speed Forever

Over more than a century of innovation, automobiles evolved from fragile three-wheeled carriages into computer-guided aerodynamic hypercars.

  1. 1886 — Benz Patent-Motorwagen: Karl Benz patented the first true internal combustion automobile in Germany, producing 0.75 horsepower.
  2. 1966 — Lamborghini Miura: Pioneered the rear mid-engine, two-seat sports car layout that defined the modern supercar blueprint.
  3. 1992 — McLaren F1: Used a full carbon-fiber monocoque chassis and gold-lined engine bay to reach 386 km/h naturally aspirated.
  4. 2019 — Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+: Became the first production hypercar to break the 300 mph (490 km/h) barrier at Ehra-Lessien.
