Bugatti Chiron Super Sport

1,600 HP 8.0L Quad-Turbo W16
440 KM/H Electronic Top Speed
1,600 NM Peak Engine Torque

The Quad-Turbo W16 Masterpiece

Founded by Ettore Bugatti in 1909 in Molsheim, France, Bugatti builds hypercars that unite extreme top-speed engineering with hand-crafted luxury.

  • 8.0-Liter W16 Powerplant: Features 16 cylinders arranged in a compact W configuration, fed by four two-stage turbochargers and ten separate radiators.
  • Longtail Aerodynamics: The Chiron Super Sport extends its rear bodywork by 25 centimeters to keep airflow attached and reduce drag at over 400 km/h.
  • Bespoke Craftsmanship: Every cockpit is hand-assembled using milled solid aluminum, exposed carbon fiber, and fine French leather.
