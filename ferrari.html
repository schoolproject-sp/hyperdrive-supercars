Formula 1 DNA for the Open Road

Born on the race tracks of Italy in 1947 under Enzo Ferrari, the Prancing Horse translates Formula 1 aerodynamics and hybrid telemetry directly into its road cars.

  • Plug-In Hybrid Architecture: Pairs a 780 CV twin-turbocharged V8 with three electric motors for instant all-wheel-drive torque vectoring.
  • Shut-Off Gurney Active Aero: A patented movable rear wing element adjusts downforce automatically during high-speed braking and cornering.
  • Fiorano Test Track Validation: Every Ferrari model is tuned lap after lap on the company's private circuit in Maranello, Italy.
