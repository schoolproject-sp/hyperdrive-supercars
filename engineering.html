Core Technologies Behind Modern Hypercars

Raw engine power alone cannot win races; hypercars rely on lightweight composites, active wings, and thermal braking systems to stay planted on tarmac.

  • Carbon-Fiber Monocoque Tubs: Woven carbon strands cured in high-pressure autoclaves create a survival cell lighter than aluminum yet stiffer than steel.
  • Venturi Tunnels & Rear Diffusers: Channels under the car accelerate airflow to create low-pressure suction, pulling the tires firmly onto the road.
  • Carbon-Ceramic Braking Systems: Resist brake fade at temperatures exceeding 1,000°C while cutting rotational unsprung weight by half.
